The curated Figure 1–4 source tables rebuild byte-for-byte from archived derived
inputs. The four final main-figure PNGs also reproduce byte-for-byte against the
existing project exports. The two latest reviewer-sensitivity plots render and
validate their full input summaries (80 rice context/universe rows; six heat
sensitivities and five generic-overlap fractions).

All selected Python programs pass syntax compilation. The four ranker entry
points provide their command-line help without raw inputs. Final figure sources
pass source preflight; all six rendered PDFs pass the 5 pt text floor and the
collision audit with zero failures and zero warnings. The main PDFs have a 6.5 pt
minimum; the two sensitivity PDFs have a 10.5 pt minimum. Comparable plot-area
groups pass physical alignment checks, with explicit heterogeneous-panel
exemptions retained. The six rendered figures were visually inspected.

These checks validate the export's derived-table and figure reproduction tier.
They do not certify a complete raw-to-result rerun, a complete R dependency lock,
or statistical independence beyond the original experimental design. Previous ED
panels outside the two latest sensitivities are not claimed to be completely
reconstructed. No package upgrade was performed for this export.
