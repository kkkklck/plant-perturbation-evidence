# Analysis overview

The primary proposition is that a target experiment's missing components determine evidence eligibility; source capability further limits defensible output resolution.

| Figure | Question | Evidence | Important limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Are strong candidates more reproducible than full response vectors? | Rice biological replicates, sixteen stress × tissue contexts, Top100 recovery and matched nulls | Candidate reproducibility does not establish full-vector fidelity |
| 2 | What transfers to an unobserved later time? | Same-state history, time-course baselines and source-matched nulls | GSE46205 numerical evidence is corrected post reveal |
| 3 | What transfers to missing states or an independent study? | Frozen heat program, held-out roots, whole-seedling target, wrong-perturbation controls | Direction agreement is conditional on overlapping candidate sets |
| 4 | Which resolution does a source support? | Five frozen stimulus groups and twenty nested targets | Five independent stimuli; geometry is confounded with response amplitude |

Top100 is the primary candidate scale. Gene/probe universes and tie handling remain those of the original scripts. Probe-set results must not be silently relabeled as independent gene-level results.

`159_build_paper_figure_tables_v2.py` assembles the four quantitative main-figure tables; it uses corrected GSE46205 sources. `164_...` and `165_...` are later reviewer sensitivities. They do not alter frozen predictions or retrospectively select the primary evidence.

Exploratory combination and mechanistic-prior results are excluded from the primary claim. Historical velocity, GRN architecture, spatial and chromatin branches are outside this repository's scientific scope.
